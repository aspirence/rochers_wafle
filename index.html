Why we made this

Snack time got boring. So we fixed the chip.

Every other pack on the shelf is the same story — maida, more maida, and a flavour that tastes like the packet it came in.

We started with whole grain jowar flour instead. Pressed it thin in a waffle iron until it cracked — not fried, just waffled — then drizzled it with real dark chocolate.

The result is a chip you actually want to eat — and one you do not have to feel odd about finishing.

What's different

Built around the grain, not around the flour.

  • Whole Grain Jowar Flour

    Sorghum is the first ingredient on the pack, not an afterthought. The grain does the heavy lifting so the chip tastes of something.

  • Zero Maida

    No refined wheat flour anywhere in the recipe. That is the entire reason this pack exists.

  • Not Fried, Just Waffled

    Pressed thin in a waffle iron instead of dropped in a fryer. That is where the clean snap comes from.

  • Real Dark Chocolate Drizzle

    Dark compound chocolate drizzled over the top, not sprayed through. You can see every line of it on the chip.

  • Gluten Free

    As printed on the pack. See the allergen note in the pack facts below before buying if you are coeliac.

  • 40g Snack Pack

    Small enough for a bag or a lunchbox, big enough to share. Pick two or four in the launch offer.
